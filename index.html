Before you go

Good to know.

Passports + rentalBring valid passports and confirm the Mercedes GLE is cleared for Canada. Keep the rental paperwork in the car.
Winter-rated tiresBC requires M+S or mountain-snowflake tires on most highways from October 1. Confirm at the rental desk.
Weather decidesCheck DriveBC before the Coquihalla, Duffey Lake Road, and any Lake Louise run. Early snow is possible.
Whidbey ferryMukilteo to Clinton is roughly 20 minutes. Vehicle fares are collected only on the island-bound crossing.
November 6 bufferAllow at least five hours from Vancouver to the flight for traffic, border wait, and the SeaTac car return.
Flight creditDelta is $1,792.80 for two, or $1,292.80 if the Delta Amex approval and $500 statement credit apply.
